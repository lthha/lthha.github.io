About

Hong Ha LE is a Ph.D. Student in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Yan Shuo Tan. Her research focuses on in-context learning for tabular data and amortized statistical inference. Before this, she obtained a B.Sc. in Applied Mathematics and a B.Sc. in Computer Science at Fulbright University Vietnam.
